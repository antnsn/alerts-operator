IMPORTANT — on every future upgrade of this chart:
  `helm upgrade` never updates the CRDs in this chart's crds/ directory once they already exist
  on the cluster (standard Helm behaviour). This project validates every CR with CEL rules baked
  into the CRDs themselves (no admission webhooks), so an un-refreshed CRD after a schema/CEL
  change means validation silently stops matching this build of the controller. Re-apply by hand
  after every upgrade:
       helm show crds antnsn/alerts-operator --version <new-version> | kubectl apply --server-side -f -
